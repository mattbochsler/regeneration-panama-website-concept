PANAMA · SCIENCE · PATIENT CARE

Independent Practice. Clear Information.

Regeneration Clinic of Panama operates in Panama’s medical environment, separate from U.S. FDA jurisdiction. The clinic describes donated umbilical-cord tissue sourcing, donor infectious-disease screening, laboratory sterility testing, personalized assessment, and follow-up care. Verify specific MINSA authorizations, clinical credentials, treatment status, and laboratory quality documentation with the company and relevant authorities.

Panama’s Medical Framework

Panama’s Ministry of Health (MINSA) and Consejo Técnico de Salud are the relevant regulatory context; no particular company permit is certified by this mockup.

Cell Sourcing & Testing

The clinic reports sourcing MSCs from donated umbilical-cord Wharton’s jelly, screening donors for infectious diseases, and performing sterility and endotoxin tests.
